Dataset & Methodology

The Bengaluru Ola Booking Dataset contains 50,000 ride records with 21 columns covering booking status, timestamps, pickup/drop locations, vehicle type, payment method, fare, ride distance, ratings, and cancellation reasons.

Analytics Pipeline

Raw CSV → Data Cleaning → Feature Engineering → Exploratory Analysis → Statistical Modeling → Business Recommendations

Key Features Engineered

  • Temporal features: Hour of day, Day of week, Weekend flag, Peak hour indicator
  • Target variable: Is_Success (1 = completed, 0 = cancelled/incomplete)
  • Label encoding for pickup/drop locations, vehicle type, payment method

Correlation Analysis

Correlation analysis reveals how key operational metrics interact. Hour of day and booking value show the clearest (though still modest) associations with ride completion and cancellation outcomes.

Temporal Demand Patterns

Ride demand in Bangalore is strongly time-dependent. Hourly ride counts rise from early morning, peak during evening commute (6-10 PM), and drop sharply at night. Weekday demand slightly exceeds weekends.

Problem 1: Cancellations & Driver Earnings Volatility

How do ride cancellations and incomplete trips affect drivers' earning stability across different times and areas?

Key Finding

Cancellations Are Concentrated

Cancellation rates spike during peak hours (evening 6-10 PM) and concentrate in high-density pickup zones. These same areas exhibit the highest volatility in driver take-home earnings, creating a double penalty: drivers face both frequent cancellations and unpredictable income.

Recommendation

Targeted Driver Incentives

Introduce dynamic driver incentives and minimum earnings guarantees in high-cancellation, high-volatility zones during peak hours. Improve driver positioning and ETA accuracy to reduce rider cancellations.

Problem 2: Payment Methods & Ride Completion

How do different payment methods affect ride completion, cancellations, and customer experience?

Key Finding

Digital Payments Perform Better

Digital payments (UPI, wallets, cards) associate with measurably higher completion rates and more consistent ratings compared to cash-based rides. Cash transactions show higher cancellation rates.

Recommendation

Nudge Toward Digital Payments

Encourage riders toward digital payment methods through in-app incentives, loyalty rewards, or default UPI/wallet selection at booking time to reduce cash-related friction and cancellations.

Problem 3: Waiting Time & Service Quality

How do waiting times (VTAT/CTAT) affect cancellations and customer ratings?

Key Finding

Waiting Time Impacts Quality

Longer VTAT/CTAT correlates with higher cancellation risk and lower customer ratings, especially in congested areas during peak hours. The effect is most visible through hourly and area-level patterns.

Recommendation

Manage Wait Times Proactively

Pre-position drivers before known peaks in high-wait zones. Provide riders with more accurate ETAs and live driver-location updates during peak windows to manage expectations and reduce frustration-driven cancellations.

Fare Modeling

An exploratory fare prediction model using Ridge Regression demonstrates that accurate fare prediction is not feasible with the publicly available dataset alone. Fares depend on dynamic factors like surge pricing, real-time traffic, and promotional discounts absent from this data.

Limitation

R² ≈ 0 — Fare Prediction Not Feasible

The Ridge Regression model achieves near-zero R², confirming that robust fare prediction requires real-time supply/demand data, surge multipliers, and traffic information not available in secondary datasets.

Findings & Recommendations

Key Finding 1

Cancellations & Earnings

Cancellation rates concentrate in specific peak hours and high-density pickup zones. These same pockets exhibit the highest volatility in driver take-home earnings, creating a reliability crisis for drivers.

Key Finding 2

Payment Methods

Digital payments (UPI, wallets) associate with measurably higher completion rates and more consistent ratings compared to cash-based rides.

Key Finding 3

Waiting Time & Quality

Longer VTAT/CTAT correlates with higher cancellation risk and lower customer ratings, especially in congested areas during peak hours.

Strategic Recommendations

Operations

Target high-volatility zones with dynamic driver incentives during peak hours. Improve ETA accuracy and driver positioning in congested pickup areas.

Product

Nudge riders toward digital payment methods through in-app incentives. Surface estimated wait times more transparently to manage rider expectations.

Drivers

Stabilize earnings in volatile areas through guaranteed minimums during identified peak windows. Provide area-specific earnings insights to help drivers choose optimal zones.

Limitations

  • Secondary dataset — no access to Ola's internal surge pricing, promotion, or routing logic
  • VTAT/CTAT fields missing for a significant portion of rides
  • Fare modeling limited by absence of contextual pricing variables
  • Geographic scope specific to Bangalore; findings may not generalize to other cities
